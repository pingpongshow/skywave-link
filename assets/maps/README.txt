Natural Earth basemap used by the Station Map window.

Source page:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-raster-data/50m-gray-earth/

Upstream asset:
https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/50m_raster/GRAY_50M_SR_W.zip

This project bundles a local PNG generated from the official GeoTIFF:
- file: `GRAY_50M_SR_W.tif`
- style: Gray Earth with Shaded Relief, Hypsography, and Flat Water
- scale: 1:50m

Local conversion:
- resized to 4096x2048
- converted to `natural_earth_gray_50m.png`

Natural Earth terms:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
